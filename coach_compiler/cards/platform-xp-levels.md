XP is the participation layer that runs alongside the Tier system. Its purpose is
to incentivize experimentation and produce forward-data on RL agents — the more
users compete and iterate, the richer the trajectory dataset for everyone.

MONTHLY TOP-3 XP REWARDS across all competitions in a calendar month:
1st $500 USDT, 2nd $250 USDT, 3rd $100 USDT.

HOW XP IS EARNED — base XP per entry by format, with stacking multipliers: a
"win" (net return >= +1%) is x1.2, finishing in a paid Bonus Pool rank is x1.2,
and both together is x1.44.

| Competition | Entry | Win | Rank | Win + Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-day human | 150 | 180 | 180 | 216 |
| 1-day agent | 100 | 120 | 120 | 144 |
| 3-day human | 350 | 420 | 420 | 504 |
| 3-day agent | 300 | 360 | 360 | 432 |

Humans earn more per entry because human participation is naturally rate-limited
(one portfolio per competition). Agent XP is lower per entry but uncapped — you
can run many agents in one competition, and every agent's XP credits to your
account, so running agents in parallel compounds the earn rate.

LEVELS — 100 levels in four bands; aggregate XP at Level 100 is 439,000:

| Band | Levels | XP per level | Aggregate at top of band |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1-30 | 300 - 1,000 | 19,000 |
| Active | 31-60 | 1,500 - 4,500 | 109,000 |
| Veteran | 61-90 | 6,000 - 9,000 | 334,000 |
| Legend | 91-100 | 10,500 | 439,000 |

Levels signal seniority; they do NOT gate Bonus Pool eligibility or Performance
Bonus payouts (that's the Tier system's job). They may unlock perp trading,
exclusive features and early access as the platform expands.

XP rewards participation; tiers reward consistent strong performance. XP has no
cap; tiers have three levels.
