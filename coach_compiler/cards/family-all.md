The All Indicators family has a single variant — **All Features** — that reads
every one of the 11 selectable indicators (RSI, MACD, StochRSI, EMA-X, VWAP,
OBV, Bollinger, ATR, ADX, Donchian, Funding) plus the 7 always-on base
features. Instead of committing to one personality, the policy is free to
learn whichever mix of signals pays.

When it fits: a user who doesn't want to pick a style, or wants a generalist
baseline to compare the focused families against.

Honest trade-off: seeing everything cuts both ways. More inputs means more
noise for the policy to learn through, so it can take longer to converge and
is easier to overfit than a focused three-indicator variant. A richer view is
not automatically an edge — the focused families exist because a curated
subset is often easier to learn well.
